# ApX Deathrun

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

CoD4x Deathrun players checking their progression, choosing customization and
comparing the current match. Players may open menus between jumps or rounds;
information must be readable quickly over a busy map at ordinary game resolutions.

## Direction

Preserve BraXi's base features and attribution while presenting the mod as ApX
Deathrun. The owner requested cleaner UI and confirmed a metallic military badge
family with increasingly elaborate ApX prestige emblems. Keep lifetime progress
when prestige resets level and XP. Avoid clutter, excessive decoration and
ambiguous destructive actions. Use text and shape in addition to team colors.
